## CurseForge summary (one line, English)

Customizable info and launcher blocks that merge like mosaic tiles: gold, bags, FPS, time, XP, professions, minimap buttons, DataBroker and more. For WoW Forever.

## GitHub "About" (repository description)

Customizable info and launcher blocks that merge like mosaic tiles, for WoW Forever (gold, bags, FPS, time, XP, professions, minimap buttons, DataBroker).

Topics: world-of-warcraft, wow-addon, wow-forever, lua, databroker

## Résumé en français (quelques lignes)

Tessera affiche de petits blocs d'informations (or, sacs, durabilité, FPS, heure, XP, métiers, courrier...) et transforme les boutons de la minicarte en blocs. Place-les où tu veux : posés les uns contre les autres, ils fusionnent comme les tesselles d'une mosaïque. Des blocs propres à WoW Forever (points d'héritage, compétences d'armes), compatible DataBroker (Titan Panel, ChocolateBar, Bazooka...), styles assortis à FlareUI, ElvUI, EllesmereUI et Blizzard, profils à exporter et partager. Pour WoW Forever, en anglais et en français.

## Short summary in English (a few lines)

Tessera shows small information blocks (gold, bags, durability, FPS, time, XP, professions, mail...) and turns minimap buttons into blocks. Place them anywhere: dropped against each other, they merge like the tiles of a mosaic. Blocks for WoW Forever's own systems (Legacy points, weapon skills), DataBroker support (Titan Panel, ChocolateBar, Bazooka...), styles matching FlareUI, ElvUI, EllesmereUI and Blizzard, profiles you can export and share. For WoW Forever, in English and French.
